Comprehensive Syllabus

What You'll Master

Direct, practical prosthodontic mastery crafted to resolve everyday chairside challenges and elevate your prosthetic standards.

Preparation Precision

Master tooth preparation protocols and customized modifications for compromised abutments to maximize retention and resistance.

Advanced FPD Designs

Learn telescopic crowns, customized coping designs, and minimally invasive resin-bonded bridges for aesthetic anterior zones.

Long-Span FPD Solutions

Understand biomechanical bars, rigid connectors, and non-rigid stress breakers to neutralize flexure in multi-unit bridges.

Material Selection

Understand definitive clinical indications, translucencies, and strength profiles for:

  • PFM (Porcelain Fused to Metal)
  • Monolithic & Layered Zirconia
  • Lithium Disilicate (E-Max)

Impression & Provisionalization

Learn double-cord gingival retraction protocols for bubble-free margins and effective laboratory communication sheets.

Cementation & Delivery

Understand chemical, resin, and bioactive luting protocols specifically formulated to eliminate debonds, remakes, and sensitivity.

Troubleshooting Failures

Identify mechanical and biologic failure etiologies early and develop actionable rescue protocols for challenging cases.

Clinical Confidence

Apply predictable, standardized, repeatable workflows to real-world clinical patient cases immediately on Monday morning.
