Prof. Masooma Aslam

Gold Medalist — Pakistan & UAE
Founder & CEO, Institute of Digital Dental Sciences

Prof. Masooma Aslam is a widely recognized dental clinician, academician, and digital dentistry educator. Having achieved top honors in Pakistan and the United Arab Emirates, her career is distinguished by extensive postgraduate training at premier medical institutions across the United Kingdom, Canada, Italy, and Pakistan.

Her clinical focus revolves around biomechanically sound fixed prosthodontics, microscopic margin preparations, advanced pediatric restorative techniques, and telemedicine in dental diagnostics. She established IDDS to ensure every dental practitioner can access master-level clinical instruction without geographic barriers.
