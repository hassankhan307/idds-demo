Institutional Profile

Excellence in Digital Dental Education

“Upgrade Your Skills. Elevate Your Practice.”
Institute of Digital Dental Sciences (IDDS) provides practicing clinicians with rigorous, case-based dental education to bridge the gap between academic textbooks and predictable chairside excellence.

Our Mission

Learn Every Dental Procedure Online to Improve Your Clinical Practice

The Institute of Digital Dental Sciences was founded to resolve a critical challenge faced by dentists worldwide: while dental materials and digital workflows advance at breakneck speeds, conventional continuing dental education remains fragmented, costly, and excessively theoretical.

IDDS eliminates this gap. By utilizing live, high-resolution interactive virtual classrooms, direct case scrutiny, and evidence-based protocols, we equip clinicians with systematic techniques they can apply with certainty the following morning.

The IDDS Standard

Clinical Accuracy Over Sales Pitches

We do not sponsor commercial brand propaganda. Our curriculum evaluates dental literature, bur geometries, luting cements, and CAD/CAM technologies with clinical objectivity and scientific rigor.

Accredited by: The CPD Group UK (#785270)
Academic Methodology

Four Pillars of IDDS Pedagogy

How our educational methodology guarantees measurable skill transformation for practicing clinicians.

01

Case-First Scrutiny

Every concept begins with an unedited clinical case, detailing the exact pre-op pathology, biological obstacles, bur choices, and laboratory communications.

02

Live Mentorship

Recorded video libraries lack interactivity. IDDS insists on live Zoom sessions where participants ask immediate questions about cases sitting in their own appointment books.

03

Digital Integration

We demystify the transition from analog alginate and elastomer trays to high-precision intraoral digital scans, STL files, and milling tolerances.

04

Accessible Pricing

Our programs are priced intentionally at PKR 5,000 (≈ $18 USD) so young dentists, residents, and established clinicians alike can access top-tier clinical mentorship.

Academic Leadership

Founded & Directed by Prof. Masooma Aslam

Gold Medalist — Pakistan & UAE

With specialized postgraduate qualifications from the British Academy (UK), McGill University (Canada), and UNICAMILLUS (Rome), Prof. Masooma Aslam established IDDS to ensure dental clinicians receive the highest caliber of evidence-based continuing education.

Her clinical philosophy merges microscopic preparation discipline with realistic laboratory coordination, empowering clinicians to protect biological structures while maximizing prosthetic longevity.
