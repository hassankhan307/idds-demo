Why Fixed Prosthodontics Mastery?

Crown and bridge restorations constitute a substantial portion of general dental practice revenue and patient satisfaction. Yet clinicians frequently battle frustrating margin exposures, open interproximal contacts, ceramic chipping, post-cementation pulpal sensitivity, and catastrophic bridge debonds.

This course removes the guesswork through evidence-based biomechanical rules, disciplined tooth preparation depths, precise cord retraction, and clear laboratory prescription workflows.

Comprehensive 12-Module Curriculum

Full Syllabus
Module 01

Diagnosis & Treatment Planning

Session 1
Clinical diagnostic protocols, smile analysis, occlusal plane evaluation, mounted study casts, diagnostic wax-ups, and risk stratification for prosthetic rehabilitation.
Module 02

Tooth Preparation Protocols

Session 1
Biomechanical principles: retention and resistance forms, total occlusal convergence (TOC 6°–12°), axial reduction depths, margin designs (deep chamfer, shoulder, knife edge), and pulp preservation techniques.
Module 03

Compromised Abutments

Session 1
Managing severely broken down crowns, post & core biomechanics (cast vs. fiber post), ferrule effect minimums, surgical crown lengthening decisions, and orthodontic extrusion indications.
Module 04

Advanced FPD Designs

Session 2
Telescopic crowns and friction-fit copings, resin-bonded Maryland bridges (single vs. double wing), preparation parameters for adhesive bridges, and anterior cantilever principles.
Module 05

Long-Span Fixed Partial Dentures (FPDs)

Session 2
Ante's Law in modern practice, deflecting forces on curved arches, rigid vs. non-rigid connectors (stress breakers), pier abutment solutions, and cross-arch splinting biomechanics.
Module 06

Material Selection (PFM, Zirconia & E-Max)

Session 2
In-depth comparative analysis: 3Y, 4Y, 5Y Zirconia translucencies vs. flexural strength; Lithium Disilicate (IPS e.max) etching and bonding; metal-ceramic indications and coping substructure design.
Module 07

Impression Techniques

Session 3
Tissue management: single vs. dual cord retraction protocols, hemostatic agents selection, addition silicone (PVS) vs. polyether handling, custom trays vs. stock trays, and intraoral digital scanning pearls.
Module 08

Provisionalization

Session 3
Direct and indirect provisional fabrication, bis-acryl resin vs. PMMA, maintaining gingival health during interim phase, emergence profile development, and temporary cements that do not interfere with final resin bonding.
Module 09

Laboratory Communication

Session 3
Writing flawless dental lab prescriptions, photography for shade matching (cross-polarization), bite registrations at correct vertical dimension (VDO), facebow transfers, and eliminating laboratory misunderstandings.
Module 10

Cementation & Delivery

Session 4
Step-by-step luting protocols: resin-modified glass ionomer (RMGI), self-adhesive resin cements, and total-etch adhesive systems. Intaglio surface treatments: silanation vs. airborne particle abrasion (MDP primers) for zirconia.
Module 11

Troubleshooting Failures

Session 4
Root-cause analysis for debonds, crown fractures, biological complications, recurrent caries, and abutment root fractures. Safe crown and bridge removal techniques without destroying preparations.
Module 12

Clinical Case Discussions & Live Q&A

Session 4
Detailed interactive walkthroughs of complex real-world patient cases presented by the faculty. Interactive critique of participant-submitted clinical cases, troubleshooting difficult margins, and final examination review.
